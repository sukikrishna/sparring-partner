# Alpha: food access at the refugee camp

A failed coup in Omega set off mass violence, and hundreds of thousands of people have crossed into neighbouring Alpha. The largest camp, about 35 km north of Alpha's capital, holds more than 160,000 refugees under roughly 700 guards. Residents need permission to leave, and the camp authorities have ties to militias. Food Without Borders (FWB) wants to distribute food in the camp and needs the Camp Commander's agreement to do it.

## If you are the FWB delegate

**Your situation.** You have just arrived as FWB's delegate for Northern Alpha and are taking over talks with the Camp Commander. Your goal is to get agreement for food distribution in the camp. Your Country Director has set one firm limit: no direct transfer of food or resources to anyone linked to security structures. Donors also expect a nutritional assessment before large-scale distribution, and they want distributions to start soon.

**Across the table.** The Camp Commander. He controls access to the camp and has insisted that guards take part in distribution and receive food as compensation. Officials and militia figures are watching closely.

**Start with these documents**

1. **Message from the FWB Country Director** (`fwb-director`). Sets out your mandate, your red line and the pressure from donors.
2. **Poor conditions in the largest refugee camp in Alpha** (`news-camp-conditions`). A baseline picture of the camp and of the allegations against the guards.
3. **Forwarded guard chat (unverified)** (`contact-note-6`). A rare glimpse of how guards talk about pay, food and the refugees. Notice how it is sourced.
4. **Leaked camp security log** (`security-log`). A long record of incidents in the camp.
5. **Message from the Mayor** (`mayor-to-fwb`). What the host community's officials expect from you.

**Questions to keep in mind**

- Officials and militia figures warn that terrorists are hiding among the refugees. What does the security log actually record, and how far can you trust a log that came to you through a third party?
- The news reports, the Mayor, the Minister of Defense and the militia leader each describe the people in the camp differently. Where do the facts differ, and who gains from each version?
- Your red line rules out *direct* transfers to security-linked actors. What does "direct" cover, and how will others on the ground see any arrangement you make?
- How do the donors' demand for an assessment and the pressure to start quickly fit together?
- Several messages come from unnamed or unverified sources. Which would you rely on?

## If you are the Camp Commander

**Your situation.** You command the camp's 160,000 refugees with about 700 guards drawn from local militias, many of them unpaid. You have to keep the camp under control, hold on to your guards' loyalty and avoid unrest or visible abuse that would embarrass Alpha's government. A new FWB delegate wants access to distribute food, and that food could ease several of your problems at once.

**Across the table.** The FWB delegate, who replaces a field officer you have already met. Behind you stand a senior militia leader, the Mayor and the Alpha government.

**Start with these documents**

1. **Role card: Camp Commander** (`commander-rolecard`). Your background, pressures and options.
2. **Minutes: FWB field officer with Camp Commander and militia leaders** (`fwb-minutes`). What happened in the last meeting with FWB, as your side recorded it.
3. **Voice message 4: senior militia leader** (`militia-voice-4`). What the militia leadership expects from distribution, in its own words.
4. **Message from the Mayor** (`mayor-to-commander`). A short and pointed demand from the capital.
5. **Refugee crisis in Alpha following unrest in Omega** (`news-refugee-crisis`). How the outside world is reporting the camp and its guards.

**Questions to keep in mind**

- The minutes record your side's account of the FWB meeting. How might the field officer have described the same meeting?
- The militia leader and your government both want things from this deal. Where do their interests line up with yours, and where do they pull apart?
- Your role card says visible abuse would be politically costly. How does that fit with what the militia leader says in the voice messages and with what the news already reports?
- The guard messages hint at a camp economy in rations and repairs. What does that suggest about what you need from FWB beyond food for the refugees?
- What can you credibly offer FWB, and what would it cost you with the guards to offer it?
