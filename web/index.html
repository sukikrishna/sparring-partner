How Sparring Partner works

Sparring Partner helps you prepare for a negotiation without writing your strategy for you.

  1. Human-Only (middle). Write your thinking in your own words, as rough as you like. The AI never edits this space.
  2. Find gaps. Passages that rest on an assumption the case documents call into question get highlighted. Each comes with a question, not a verdict.
  3. Source Documents (right). Click a highlight to open the documents that bear on it, with a separate chat for each. You can also browse the whole library or ask a question across all documents.
  4. AI-Synthesis (left). Key findings collect on a board, labelled with where they came from. Add your own notes there too. The structured view restates your notes in negotiation terms (interests, island of agreement, red lines, alternatives) and shows what is still open.
  5. Revise. Edit a highlighted passage and press “Submit changes”. If it no longer rests on the assumption, the gap closes; your findings stay.

Scripted demo uses prepared answers for one scenario (Qiso, as the Mayor) and passage search elsewhere. Live uses a language model through the Python server and works on any case and any notes.
