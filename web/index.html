    How Sparring Partner works

    Sparring Partner helps you understand a negotiation case and test your own reasoning against it. It does not write your strategy for you.

    1. Write in Your strategy notes (middle), in your own words, as rough as you like. The AI never edits this space.
    2. Find gaps. Press the button and passages that rest on an assumption the case documents call into question get highlighted, each with a question rather than a verdict.
    3. Investigate in Source Documents (right). Click a highlight to open the documents behind it, each with its own chat. You can also browse the whole library or ask across all documents.
    4. Collect in AI-Synthesis (left). Each gap gets a section there, and findings from the chats are saved under it automatically (or after you confirm), each labelled with its source. Add your own notes too. The structured view restates your notes in negotiation terms.
    5. Revise. Rewrite a highlighted passage and press “Find gaps” again. Highlights you have dealt with clear; any new ones appear.

    Scripted demo uses prepared answers for one scenario (Qiso, as the Mayor) and passage search elsewhere. Live uses a language model through the Python server and works on any case and any notes.
