How Sparring Partner works

Sparring Partner helps you understand a negotiation case and test your own reasoning against it. It does not write your strategy for you.

  1. Write in the Human-Only column (middle), in your own words, as rough as you like. The AI never edits this space.
  2. Find gaps. Passages that rest on an assumption the case documents call into question get highlighted. Each comes with a question, not a verdict.
  3. Investigate in Source Documents (right). Click a highlight to open the documents behind it, each with its own chat. You can also browse the whole library or ask across all documents.
  4. Collect in AI-Synthesis (left). Findings gather on a board, each labelled with its source; add your own notes there too. The structured view restates your notes in negotiation terms and shows what is still open.
  5. Revise. Edit a highlighted passage and press “Submit changes”. If it no longer rests on the assumption, the gap closes; your findings stay.

Scripted demo uses prepared answers for one scenario (Qiso, as the Mayor) and passage search elsewhere. Live uses a language model through the Python server and works on any case and any notes.
